What Is Whole Life Insurance

Coverage That Lasts as Long as You Do

Whole life insurance is permanent coverage — unlike term policies, it never expires as long as you pay premiums. Your beneficiaries are guaranteed a death benefit no matter when you pass away.

Beyond the death benefit, whole life builds cash value over time at a guaranteed rate. That cash value grows tax-deferred and can be borrowed against for any purpose — retirement income, emergencies, or major expenses.
