TCPA Compliance &
Communication Consent

Our standards for contacting you and your rights under the Telephone Consumer Protection Act.

Effective Date: April 28, 2026  ·  Last Updated: April 28, 2026

1. What Is the TCPA?

The Telephone Consumer Protection Act (47 U.S.C. § 227) is a federal law that governs how businesses may contact consumers by phone call, SMS/text message, and fax. The TCPA requires businesses to obtain prior express written consent before contacting consumers using an automatic telephone dialing system (autodialer) or a prerecorded voice message.

Engel Financial Group is committed to full compliance with the TCPA and all applicable FCC regulations, including the 2025 consent revocation rules that took effect on April 11, 2025.

2. Our Consent Standard

When you submit a form on this website, you will see the following disclosure directly above the submit button:

"By submitting this form, you consent to be contacted by Engel Financial Group and its licensed representatives by phone call, SMS/text message, and email regarding insurance products and services. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase."

Submitting the form constitutes your prior express written consent under the TCPA. Each consent record is logged with the following data:

  • The exact disclosure language presented to you at the time of submission
  • Timestamp of submission (recorded to the second)
  • IP address of the submitting device
  • Source URL (the specific page where the form was submitted)

3. How We Contact You

  • Phone calls — made by a live licensed agent only; we do not use prerecorded or artificial voice messages without your separate prior consent
  • SMS/text messages — sent only to numbers provided via form submission; limited to messages related to your insurance inquiry
  • Email — used to deliver quote information and follow-up on your inquiry

We contact you solely regarding insurance products and services relevant to your inquiry. We do not use your contact information for unrelated third-party marketing.

4. Your Right to Opt Out

You may revoke consent and opt out of any or all communication methods at any time:

SMS / Text
Reply STOP to any text message from us
Email
Click Unsubscribe in any email we send
Phone
Request removal verbally during any call
All Methods
privacy@engelfinancialgroup.com

All opt-out requests are processed within 10 business days, in accordance with FCC rules effective April 2025. Once your request is processed, you will not receive further communications through the requested channel(s).

5. Record Keeping

Engel Financial Group retains consent records for a minimum of 2 years from the date of collection. Each record includes the disclosure language shown, a precise timestamp, the IP address of the submitting device, and the source URL. These records are maintained to demonstrate compliance in the event of a regulatory inquiry or legal proceeding.

6. Contact for TCPA Concerns

If you have questions about our TCPA practices, believe you have received a communication in error, or wish to submit an opt-out request, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@engelfinancialgroup.com
  • Engel Financial Group — Independent Life Insurance Broker

We take TCPA compliance seriously and will address all concerns promptly.
