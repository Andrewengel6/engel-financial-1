What Is Mortgage Protection

Your Mortgage Disappears. Your Family Stays Home.

Mortgage protection insurance is a life insurance policy designed specifically to pay off your outstanding mortgage balance if you die. Your family receives the home, free and clear, without the burden of monthly payments during one of the hardest times of their lives.

Unlike traditional life insurance where the benefit goes to your beneficiary to use as they choose, mortgage protection is structured to cover the exact amount of your outstanding loan — ensuring the most important financial obligation your family has is completely handled.
