5. Placement Approach

Advantage+ Placements — On, but Asset-Specific

Use Advantage+ Placements at launch so Meta can allocate across placements based on where conversions are cheapest. However: provide placement-specific assets for every key placement. Do not rely on a single 1:1 or 4:5 asset being auto-cropped into Reels — the result is letterboxed, feels amateur, and burns CPM budget. Upload the 9:16 master for Reels/Stories and the 4:5 adapted version for Feed. Advantage+ will pick the right asset per placement automatically.

⚠ Never upload only a square or landscape asset and let Meta auto-crop into 9:16. It destroys the hook frame and pushes critical text into UI zones. Always provide the correct native format per placement.
6. Video Creative Requirements
⚡
Hook in First 2 Seconds
The hook line must be on screen and spoken within the first 2 seconds. Text overlay of the hook appears from frame 1. Reels users swipe at 2–3 sec if not hooked.
Example frame 1: "Don't leave your family with a question mark." — full screen text + avatar speaking
💬
Captions / On-Screen Text Required
85% of Reels are watched without sound. Every spoken word must have a caption or on-screen text equivalent. Auto-captions are acceptable if reviewed for accuracy.
Style: white text, dark outline or semi-transparent bg — never bare white on white or light backgrounds
🤖
AI Avatar — Advisor-Led Feel
Avatar must feel natural and credible — not robotic or generic. Talking-head style, direct eye contact with camera, calm and professional. Not a voiceover over stock footage.
Avatar: mid-40s appearance, professional casual attire, neutral home-office or clean background
🏷
Brand Visible Early
Engel Financial Group name or logo visible within first 3 seconds. Can be subtle — lower-third text or small logo watermark. Builds trust signal before the hook lands.
Position: lower-left or lower-right · Avoid bottom 250px on 9:16 (Meta UI zone)
📋
CTA Pre-frames the Review
CTA must clearly state this starts a short life insurance review — not a quote, not a sale. "Start your free review" or "Check your options in 5 minutes." Removes surprise at the wizard.
Spoken CTA + on-screen text CTA — both present in the final 3 seconds
📱
Safe Zone — No Text Near Edges
Keep all critical text and faces within the safe zone. No important content in bottom 250px (UI bar), top 150px (profile/share UI), or within 50px of left/right edges.
9:16 safe zone: 980px wide · top 150px to bottom 250px clear
🧑‍💼
Agent Follow-Up Pre-Frame
Video must mention that a licensed advisor will reach out after the review is complete. Spoken or on-screen. Reduces surprise, improves answer rate, pre-qualifies intent.
Example: "After your review, a licensed advisor from Engel Financial Group will reach out to walk through your options."
⏱
Recommended Length
15–30 seconds for Reels/Stories cold traffic. 30–45 seconds acceptable for retargeting warm audience who already know the brand. Keep it tight — every second must earn its place.
Launch with 15–20 sec versions · Test longer cuts only after baseline CPL is established
