What's the primary emotional angle?

The dominant hook shapes all headlines, body copy, and creative direction. Pick the one that feels most "you."

A

Protection / Fear of Loss

"What happens to your family if something happens to you?"

Leads with the risk. Speaks to parents, homeowners, breadwinners. Strong emotional urgency. Highest click-through for cold audiences — people feel the stakes immediately.

Best for: Ages 35–55, homeowners with dependents, primary earners

B

Legacy / Leaving Something Behind

"Your family will remember you forever. Make sure they're taken care of, too."

Leads with pride and purpose. Speaks to people who think long-term — parents, grandparents, homeowners building generational wealth. Less fear, more aspiration.

Best for: Ages 45–65, family-oriented, thinking about the future

C

Cash Value / Financial Asset

"Life insurance that builds real wealth — not just coverage."

Leads with the financial upside. Speaks to savers, investors, and business owners who see whole life as an asset, not a cost. Differentiates from term insurance strongly.

Best for: Ages 35–55, financially minded, comparing coverage options
