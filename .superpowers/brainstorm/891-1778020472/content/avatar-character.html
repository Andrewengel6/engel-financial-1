Avatar Character Direction

Who does your audience trust most? Pick the character archetype that will get the most opt-ins from cold Meta traffic — homeowners, 35–55, family-focused, 15 states.

👔

A — The Local Advisor

Early-to-mid 40s. Authoritative, trustworthy, professional.

Appearance

Light blazer over open-collar dress shirt. Well-groomed, confident posture. Looks like a local financial planner or licensed insurance broker. Clean home office or neutral wall background.

Feel

Someone you'd trust to give you straight financial advice. Authority without arrogance. Has clearly done this before.

Best for angles

Angle 2 (Coverage Options) — "Not all life insurance works the same way."

🏡

B — The Relatable Family Man

Late 30s to mid 40s. Peer-to-peer energy. Looks like the audience.

Appearance

Open-collar casual shirt (no blazer). Put-together but not corporate. Could be a homeowner, a dad, a guy who figured out he needed to protect his family and is sharing what he learned. Warm, neutral home background.

Feel

Not selling — sharing. Feels like a friend who has already figured this out. High relatability for cold Meta traffic. Lower authority, higher trust.

Best for angles

Angle 1 (Family Protection) + Angle 4 (Peace of Mind / Guidance)

💼

C — The Confident Problem-Solver

Early 40s to early 50s. Direct, clear, no-nonsense.

Appearance

Business casual — fitted shirt or smart polo, no tie. Strong direct eye contact. Slight lean-in posture. Looks like someone who cuts through confusion and gives real answers fast. Neutral light background.

Feel

Calm authority. "I know what you need and I'm going to tell you clearly." Stops the scroll because they seem like they actually know what they're talking about.

Best for angles

Angle 3 (Mortgage/Homeowner) + Angle 4 (Guidance) — "Life insurance does not have to feel confusing."
