Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Effective Date: April 28, 2026  ·  Last Updated: April 28, 2026

1. Introduction

Engel Financial Group ("we," "our," or "us") is an independent life insurance brokerage subject to applicable state and federal financial services regulations, including the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect personal information when you visit engelfinancialgroup.com or submit an inquiry through our contact forms.

By using this website, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use this site.

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • First and last name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Coverage type of interest
  • Any message or notes you submit via our contact form

Information Collected Automatically

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referring URL (how you arrived at our site)
  • Timestamps of form submissions and site interactions
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to improve site functionality and measure advertising performance. This includes first-party session cookies and the Meta Pixel (see Section 4).

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your insurance inquiries and deliver quote information
  • To connect you with licensed insurance carriers appropriate to your coverage needs
  • To improve the performance and usability of our website
  • To run and optimize advertising campaigns on Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and other platforms
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations under GLBA, TCPA, and applicable state law
  • To maintain consent records as required by the Telephone Consumer Protection Act

4. Meta Pixel & Tracking Technologies

This website uses the Meta Pixel (also known as the Facebook Pixel), a tracking tool provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. The Pixel allows us to measure the effectiveness of our advertising, build custom audiences for retargeting, and track conversions from our Meta ad campaigns on Facebook and Instagram.

Data the Meta Pixel may collect: page views, button clicks, form interactions, IP address, browser fingerprint, and behavioral data about your visit to this site. This data is transmitted to Meta Platforms, Inc., 1 Hacker Way, Menlo Park, CA 94025, USA.

Your Opt-Out Options

  • Manage your Meta ad preferences: facebook.com/ads/preferences
  • Review Meta's data policy: facebook.com/privacy/policy
  • Use your browser's privacy settings or an extension such as uBlock Origin to block tracking pixels
  • Opt out of interest-based advertising at optout.aboutads.info

Meta's use of data collected via the Pixel is governed by Meta's Data Policy, which is independent of this Privacy Policy.

We may also use contact information you submit through our forms — in hashed, non-reversible format — to create suppression audiences with advertising platforms such as Meta, so that individuals who have already completed a review do not continue to receive ads. This data is transmitted in hashed form only and is not shared with Meta in a way that allows them to identify you independently.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

We may share your information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Licensed insurance carriers — solely to fulfill your request for a quote or policy information
  • Meta Platforms, Inc. — via the Meta Pixel as described in Section 4
  • Service providers — vendors who assist with website operations, under confidentiality agreements prohibiting secondary use of your data
  • Legal requirements — if required by law, court order, or regulatory request from a government authority

6. GLBA Privacy Notice

As a financial services entity subject to the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act, we are required to notify you about our information-sharing practices. We collect nonpublic personal information as described in Section 2. We do not share your nonpublic personal information with unaffiliated third parties for their own marketing purposes. For questions about our GLBA compliance, contact us at privacy@engelfinancialgroup.com.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your state of residence, you may have the following rights:

  • Right to know — request a summary of the personal information we hold about you
  • Right to deletion — request that we delete your information (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Right to correction — request correction of inaccurate data we hold about you
  • Right to opt out — opt out of certain data sharing or targeted advertising practices

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), including the right to know about personal information disclosed for business purposes. Although Engel Financial Group is not licensed to sell insurance in California, California residents may visit this site and are entitled to these protections.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@engelfinancialgroup.com. We will respond within 45 days of receiving your request.

8. Do Not Sell / Do Not Share

We do not sell or rent your personal information to any third party for their marketing purposes. This applies to all visitors regardless of state of residence.

To limit data transmission to Meta via the Pixel, use Meta Ad Preferences at facebook.com/ads/preferences or adjust your browser privacy settings.

9. Data Retention

  • Form submissions — retained for 3 years for follow-up and compliance purposes
  • TCPA consent records — retained for a minimum of 2 years; each record includes the disclosure language shown, timestamp (to the second), IP address, and source URL
  • Deletion requests — submitted to privacy@engelfinancialgroup.com; processed within 45 days, subject to any legal retention obligations

10. Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational safeguards to protect your personal information, including HTTPS encryption for all data transmitted to and from this site, and access controls limiting staff access to personal data. No method of internet transmission is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security but commit to reasonable protective measures.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in an updated effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of this website following any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

12. SMS Messaging & Text Communications

When you submit a form on this website, you consent to receive SMS text messages and phone calls from Engel Financial Group at the number you provide. These messages may include responses to your inquiry, appointment confirmations, and information about insurance products and services.

Message Frequency & Rates

Message frequency may vary depending on your inquiry and the nature of our correspondence. Standard message and data rates may apply based on your mobile carrier plan.

How to Opt Out

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive from us. After opting out, you will receive one final confirmation message and no further SMS messages will be sent. To re-subscribe, reply START at any time.

Help

For assistance, reply HELP to any message or contact us at privacy@engelfinancialgroup.com or call (501) 691-5508.

Carrier Disclaimer

Engel Financial Group is not responsible for any delays in the delivery of SMS messages, as delivery is subject to effective transmission by your mobile carrier. Message and data rates may apply.

SMS Data & Privacy

Phone numbers collected through our forms are used solely to respond to your inquiry and are never sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes. SMS consent is not a condition of purchase. See our data retention practices in Section 9 of this policy.
