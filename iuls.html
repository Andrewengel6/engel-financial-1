What Is an IUL

Upside Potential. Downside Protection.

An Indexed Universal Life (IUL) policy ties your cash value growth to a stock market index — like the S&P 500 — without directly investing in the market. When the index goes up, you earn interest. When it goes down, your floor protects you from losses.

Because growth accumulates tax-deferred and distributions can be taken as tax-free policy loans, IULs are increasingly used as a tax-free retirement income strategy — especially for high earners who have maxed out traditional accounts.
